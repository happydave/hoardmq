This is a stub policy. It will be expanded before the first public release.

[LICENSE-MIT](LICENSE-MIT) and [LICENSE-APACHE](LICENSE-APACHE)). Those
licences cover the code. They do not grant rights to the name "HoardMQ" or to
any HoardMQ logo.

- You may use the name HoardMQ to refer to this software, including in
  unmodified redistributions and in factual statements such as "built on
  HoardMQ" or "compatible with HoardMQ".
- A fork or a modified distribution must not present itself as HoardMQ. Give it
  its own name and state that it is derived from HoardMQ.
- Do not use the name in a way that suggests endorsement by, or affiliation
  with, the HoardMQ project.
- The name is not registered as a trademark. The project relies on common-law
  rights from public distribution and coexists with unrelated uses of the word
  "Hoard".

Questions about naming can be raised as an issue on the project repository.
